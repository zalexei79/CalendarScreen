DAYRIS PRO — MetaTrader 5

This connector is available to every signed-in user with active DAYRIS PRO. Each user installs the exporter in their own terminal and imports into their own DAYRIS account. No account identifiers, passwords, keys or machine-specific paths are built into the exporter.

1. Sign in to your existing broker account in MetaTrader 5.
2. In DAYRIS > Platforms > MetaTrader 5, download DAYRIS_MT5.ex5.
3. MT5 > File > Open Data Folder > MQL5 > Experts: copy DAYRIS_MT5.ex5 here. In Navigator, right-click Expert Advisors > Refresh. Attach DAYRIS_MT5 to ONE chart.
4. The exporter only reads history and writes a file. It has no order, network or DLL calls. Trading permission, passwords and API keys are not required.
5. In desktop Chrome/Edge, open DAYRIS > Platforms > MetaTrader 5 > Choose DAYRIS folder. Select:
   %APPDATA%\MetaQuotes\Terminal\Common\Files\DAYRIS
6. Review the import count and confirm importing into your current DAYRIS account. If the account has no closed positions, confirm connection with zero entries. Enable refresh every 30 seconds.
7. Keep MT5 and DAYRIS open on the computer. Closing the connection dialog does not stop updates; closing/reloading the website does. Choose the folder again after reload. Sign-out, account change or loss of PRO access stops the connector.
8. Saved trades reach your other devices through existing DAYRIS cloud sync. Mobile/Safari/Firefox cannot read a PC folder automatically; use CSV import or sync first on a PC.
9. MT5 fully closed positions only: partial closes are combined once the whole position is closed. Profit, swap, entry/exit commissions and fees are included. Deposits/withdrawals are excluded. Account-level adjustments posted separately are not allocated to positions.
10. Dates use broker time. Repeat imports skip the server/account/position marker in the comment. Preserve this marker. Broker corrections to already imported records are not applied automatically. Use one DAYRIS window per trading account to avoid concurrent duplicate imports.
11. MT4 is experimental source only and has not been compiled or tested with an MT4 terminal. It is not offered as a ready production connection.

РУССКИЙ
Подключение доступно каждому пользователю с активным PRO, отдельно для его собственного счёта.
Скачайте готовый DAYRIS_MT5.ex5 в DAYRIS. В MT5 откройте «Файл > Открыть каталог данных > MQL5 > Experts» и скопируйте файл туда. Обновите «Советники» в Навигаторе и прикрепите DAYRIS_MT5 к одному графику.
В DAYRIS выберите папку %APPDATA%\MetaQuotes\Terminal\Common\Files\DAYRIS, подтвердите импорт в текущий аккаунт и включите автообновление. Подключение возможно и до первой закрытой позиции.
Сайт и терминал должны оставаться открытыми на компьютере. После перезагрузки сайта выберите папку заново. На телефон сохранённые сделки попадут через облачную синхронизацию DAYRIS.
Пароли и ключи не нужны. Торговые разрешения и DLL включать для экспортёра не требуется. MT4 пока не проверен.
